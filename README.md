# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project analyzing **3,900 customer purchases** to understand shopping patterns, customer segments, product preferences, discounts, subscriptions, and revenue drivers.

## 🎯 Objective

Identify customer behavior trends and provide insights to improve:

- Customer engagement
- Customer loyalty
- Marketing strategies
- Product positioning
- Subscription adoption

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Python** – Data cleaning and feature engineering
- **SQL / MySQL** – Business analysis
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard
- **Pandas, NumPy, SQLAlchemy, PyMySQL**
- **Git & GitHub** – Version control

## 🔄 Project Workflow

```text
Raw Dataset
     ↓
Python
Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering
     ↓
MySQL
Business Analysis & Customer Segmentation
     ↓
Power BI
Dashboard & Visualization
     ↓
Business Insights & Recommendations
```

## 📊 Dataset

- **3,900** purchase records
- **18** columns
- Customer demographics
- Product and purchase information
- Discounts and promotions
- Review ratings
- Subscription status
- Shipping preferences
- Purchase frequency

Missing values in `review_rating` were handled using category-level median values.

Additional features such as `age_group` and `purchase_frequency_days` were created during the data preparation process.

## 🔍 Key Analysis

The project answers questions such as:

- Which customer groups generate the most revenue?
- Which products have the highest ratings?
- Which products depend heavily on discounts?
- Do subscribers behave differently from non-subscribers?
- How does shipping type relate to spending?
- Which products perform best within each category?
- How can customers be segmented based on purchase history?
- How can repeat purchases and customer loyalty be improved?

## 📈 Power BI Dashboard

The dashboard provides insights into:

- Total customers
- Average purchase amount
- Average review rating
- Revenue by category
- Sales by category
- Revenue by age group
- Sales by age group
- Subscription analysis
- Interactive filters for customer and purchase attributes

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- **Boost Subscriptions** – Promote exclusive subscriber benefits.
- **Strengthen Loyalty** – Reward repeat customers.
- **Optimize Discounts** – Balance promotional sales with profitability.
- **Improve Product Positioning** – Highlight highly rated and popular products.
- **Targeted Marketing** – Focus campaigns on high-value customer segments.
